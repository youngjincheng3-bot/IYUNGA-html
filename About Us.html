About Iyunga Technical Secondary School.

Welcome to Iyunga Technical Secondary School,
A premier government technical institution dedicated to nurturing technical expertise,
scientific innovation, and academic excellence. Located in the Mbeya Region of Tanzania,
Iyunga has stood as a center for practical education, equipping young minds with the skills necessary to excel in higher education
and the modern engineering workforce

HeadMaster's Welcome😀😀 Message.

"At Iyunga Technical Secondary School, we believe that education extends beyond the textbook.
Our mission is to transform curiosity into practical skill. Through rigorous technical workshops, modern science laboratories, and strong discipline,
we prepare our students to become the engineers, innovators, and leaders of tomorrow."

Our History & Heritage.

Founded as part of Tanzania’s strategic vision to expand technical and scientific education,
Iyunga Technical Secondary School has grown into one of the country's prominent technical institutions.
Operating under the Ministry of State in the President's Office - Regional Administration and Local Government (TAMISEMI),
the school serves male students from across the nation for both Ordinary Level (O-Level) and Advanced Level (A-Level)
studies. Over the decades, Iyunga has continuously modernized its workshops and learning environment,
earning a reputation for strong academic performance in national NECTA examinations and outstanding technical skill

Our Vision🤷‍♂️

To be a center of excellence in technical education, training, and innovation,
fostering skilled and disciplined future leaders for national development

Our Mission 🌍.

To provide high-quality secondary and technical education through practical training,
academic rigor, character building, and innovative problem-solving.
